# AUTHOR PROFILE — BASELINE

> This is the author's stylistic profile. Update iteratively based on reality. Sections marked `[EDIT]` should be personalized.

## 1. Communication Baseline

- Conversational interaction language: Vietnamese (or user-initiated language).
- Target report/document language: Specified per project or section (e.g., Vietnamese, English).
- Presentation style: Direct, concise, technical, immediately addressing mechanics and problems.
- Dislikes: Marketing fluff, empty academic jargon, padded answers intended to look impressive.
- Priorities: Technical accuracy, empirical evidence, reproducibility, verifiability.
- When uncertain: Explicitly state unknown / needs verification; never guess or hallucinate.

## 2. Research Personality

- Hands-on, implementation-focused.
- Insists on knowing: "Based on what?", "Which log/metric?", "Under what configuration?", "How was it measured?".
- Skeptical of absolute or unhedged claims.
- Demands that reports reflect what was actually implemented and observed, not idealized expectations.

## 3. Desired Voice

Voice should reflect an active, pragmatic engineer or researcher:
- Calm and deliberate;
- Methodical and cautious;
- Concrete and specific;
- Free of linguistic posturing or artificial intellectualism;
- Readily candid about constraints and shortcomings;
- Uses precise technical terminology without keyword stuffing.

## 4. Academic Culture & Tone Conventions

- Avoid exaggerating student/project contributions.
- Use scoped claims: *"Within our test environment"*, *"Under configuration X"*, *"Across dataset Y"*.
- Maintain intellectual modesty regarding results.
- Zero promotional or vendor-brochure language.
- Address limitations head-on with technical objectivity.

## 5. Sentence Construction Preferences

Preferred:
`X employs Y to accomplish Z under condition A.`
(Vietnamese equivalent: `X sử dụng Y để thực hiện Z trong điều kiện A.`)

Avoid:
`X delivers a powerful, flexible, and groundbreaking capability to revolutionize Z...`
(Vietnamese equivalent: `X mang đến một khả năng mạnh mẽ, linh hoạt và hiệu quả nhằm hỗ trợ Z...`)
